# Clips needed (record in GTA 6 and send)

## How to record (all clips)
- **16:9, 1080p or higher** (1440p/4K even better), 30 or 60 fps.
- **3-8 seconds each** (a little extra is fine, I trim).
- **No facecam, no voice, no music** on top (game sound is OK, I mute it).
- **Character in the middle of the screen** where possible.
- **HUD ON** only where the HUD is the point (stars, minimap, menus).
  Otherwise HUD OFF / cinematic camera looks better.
- Daytime / bright scenes look best (unless the line says night).
- **File name = reel + number + short name**, e.g. `r03_01_six_stars.mp4`.
